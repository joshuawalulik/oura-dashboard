Privacy Policy

This is a private, personal dashboard operated by its owner for exactly two people: the owner and their partner.

  • It displays health summary data (readiness, sleep, and activity scores, sleep duration, bedtime, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, body temperature deviation, and readiness contributor scores) fetched from the owner's own Oura account via the Oura API.
  • Access is restricted to two named email addresses behind Cloudflare Access. No other person can view the data.
  • Data is cached briefly (up to 3 hours) to reduce API calls.
  • A daily history of those fields, apart from the readiness contributor scores, and the summaries computed from it, is kept in the site's private Cloudflare storage so trends can be compared over time. It remains there after the Oura account is disconnected, until the owner deletes it.
  • Data is not sold or shared with anyone.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
