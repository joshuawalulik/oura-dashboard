Privacy Policy

This is a private, personal dashboard operated by its owner for exactly two people: the owner and their partner.

  • It displays health summary data (readiness, sleep, and activity scores and sleep duration) fetched from the owner's own Oura account via the Oura API.
  • Access is restricted to two named email addresses behind Cloudflare Access. No other person can view the data.
  • Data is cached briefly (up to 3 hours) to reduce API calls and is not otherwise stored, archived, sold, or shared with anyone.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
