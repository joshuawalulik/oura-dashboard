# Surface: the dashboard (public/index.html)

Mode: Operate. A private night-table readout for two people, glanced at on an iPhone in dim light. Success: today's readiness answer lands in under a second; nothing alarms; everything stays legible at arm's length in the dark.

User decisions (2026-11-03): status colors get an amber floor at instrument-lamp intensity (no alarm red); color encodes metric identity everywhere (readiness=copper, sleep=blue, activity=olive) with status carried by a word + small tick, never by recoloring the metric; disconnect demoted below the legal links, quiet at rest; full scope (visual redo + all P1/P2 + persona/minor fixes).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A cockpit instrument for sleep — the night-flight deck. One glance reads the day like a pilot reads an altimeter. Refuses the category default (three equal stat cards + stock chart block) and its opposite (glowing neon "health tech").

OWN-WORLD: Graphite flight-deck panels (#0b0c0e ground, #15171a panels) with hairline bezels; a single burnished-copper accent; B612/B612 Mono (the Airbus cockpit face, self-hosted) as the only voices; metric hues: copper (readiness), dim blue (sleep), olive (activity); status as a desaturated word, never a floodlight. Recognizable with all content removed by its 270° tick-gauge dials.

STORY: Josh or his partner wakes, glances: the big dial answers "how recovered", the two small dials and the sleep time fill in the picture, the trends confirm the week. Nothing asks for action; the one button that destroys (disconnect) rests last and quiet.

FIRST VIEWPORT (390px): header (title + updated stamp, AA-legible), then the readiness dial at full card width — 270° arc with SVG tick ring, mono numeral, status word — above a 2-up row of smaller sleep/activity dials, then the time-asleep stat row. All within the first screen.

FORM: 270° tick-gauge dial, evolved from the committed instrument world (pinned brief: evolve the incumbent, dark/calm/data-first). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
